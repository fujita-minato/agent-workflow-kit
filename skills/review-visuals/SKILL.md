# Review visuals

Inspect the intended delivery format. Watch the complete candidate at normal speed. For visual tutorials, mute it and follow the actual sequence, checking that the starting state, target, gesture and result are recognizable. Watch with sound for sync and intelligibility when relevant.

Check the destination safe area, focal point, text repetition, visibility and timing by the project's criteria. Use frames to locate an issue and playback to verify motion. Record exact timestamps and the independent viewer's interpretation. Give PASS, REVISE or BLOCKED and bind the evidence to the candidate SHA-256.

Use the local fingerprint and evidence format in the deliver-revision skill. If you cannot open the exact file in an approved local player, tell the project lead to assign an independent local viewer. The viewer supplies the playback verdict. Treat missing playback as BLOCKED. Keep media and notes local.
