# Review content

Freeze the intended audience and candidate file before reading. For a film, compare the spoken and visible claims against their sources. Check that quantities, limitations, factual conditions and examples survive the revision. Treat a transcript or a previous version as supporting evidence only.

Try to answer the promised question as the intended audience. Identify the exact phrase, time or visual claim that affects the answer. Give PASS, REVISE or BLOCKED. Tie the evidence to the candidate SHA-256. When a change affects previous conclusions, review those conclusions again.
